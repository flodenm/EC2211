## 1. GDP accounting

Which expression is the national income identity?

- **A.** $Y - IM = C + I + G$
- **B.** $Y + EX = C + I + G + IM$
- **C.** $Y - EX = C + I + G$
- **D.** $Y + IM = C + I + G + EX$

## 2. Nominal and real GDP

Nominal GDP and the GDP deflator both increase by 10 percent. What happens to real GDP?

- **A.** It increases by approximately 20 percent.
- **B.** It increases by 10 percent.
- **C.** It remains unchanged.
- **D.** It decreases by 10 percent.

## 3. Constant returns to scale

Which **two** of the following production functions display constant returns to scale?

- **A.** $Y = F(K,L) = K^{1/2} + L^{1/2}$
- **B.** $y = f(k) = k$
- **C.** $Y = F(K,L) = KL$
- **D.** $Y = F(K,L) = K^{1/2}L^{1/2}$
- **E.** $Y = F(K,H,L) = K^{1/2}H^{1/2}L^{1/2}$

## 4. Growth accounting

Output per capita grows by 2 percent and capital per capita grows by 3 percent. If the capital share is 1/3, approximately how fast does TFP grow?

- **A.** 0 percent
- **B.** 1 percent
- **C.** 2 percent
- **D.** 3 percent

## 5. Solow model

Which equation best describes how the capital stock develops over time in the Solow model?

- **A.** $K_{t+1} = K_t + sAK_t^\alpha L_t^{1-\alpha} - \delta K_t$
- **B.** $K_{t+1} = (1-\delta)K_t + Y_t + I_t$
- **C.** $K_{t+1} = (1-\delta)Y_t + I_t$
- **D.** $K_{t+1} = (1-\delta)K_t + sI_t$

## 6. A poverty trap

At which level of capital will the economy end up if initial capital is $K_3$? If initial capital is $K_5$?

- **A.** $K_3 \longrightarrow K_4$ and $K_5 \longrightarrow K_6$
- **B.** $K_3 \longrightarrow K_2$ and $K_5 \longrightarrow K_4$
- **C.** $K_3 \longrightarrow K_4$ and $K_5 \longrightarrow K_4$
- **D.** $K_3 \longrightarrow K_2$ and $K_5 \longrightarrow K_6$

### Graph description

The graph appears on page 2 of the source PDF.

- The horizontal axis is capital, $K$. The labelled capital levels are ordered $K_1 < K_2 < K_3 < K_4 < K_5 < K_6$.
- The orange output curve, $Y$, rises, flattens, rises steeply again and then flattens.
- The green saving/investment curve, $sY$, has the same general shape at a lower level.
- The blue depreciation line, $\delta K$, is a straight upward-sloping line through the origin.
- The $sY$ curve and $\delta K$ line intersect at $K_2$, $K_4$ and $K_6$.
- For positive capital below $K_2$, $sY$ is above $\delta K$. Between $K_2$ and $K_4$, $sY$ is below $\delta K$. Between $K_4$ and $K_6$, $sY$ is above $\delta K$. Beyond $K_6$, $sY$ is below $\delta K$.
