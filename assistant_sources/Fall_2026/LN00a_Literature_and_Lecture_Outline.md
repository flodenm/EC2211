## Preliminary lecture outline

### Lecture outline

L1. **Measuring the economy**

- Is Europe lagging behind?
- National accounts; prices and quantities; productivity
- Literature: Jones, chapters 1-3
- Recommended reading: [Draghi (2024, pages 5-19)](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/97e481fd-2dc3-412d-be4c-f152a8232961_en?filename=The%20future%20of%20European%20competitiveness%20_%20A%20competitiveness%20strategy%20for%20Europe.pdf); [Bunel et al. (2025)](https://www.banque-france.fr/system/files/2025-02/391_Revisiting_the_european_performance_gap_vis-a-vis_the_united_states.pdf)

L2. **Economic growth and the Solow model**

- Can investment generate permanently higher economic growth?
- Physical capital and production; growth accounting; the Solow model
- Literature: Jones, chapters 4-5

### Lecture outline cont'd.

L3. **Growth and development in the Solow model**

- Why do countries have different levels of output per worker?
- Population growth; technological progress; human capital; convergence; exhaustible resources
- Literature: Jones, chapter 5, section 7.7

L4. **Ideas, institutions and long-run growth**

- Where does sustianed growth come from?
- The Romer model; growth accounting; institutions and growth
- Literature: Jones, chapter 6; [*Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences (2024)](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2024/10/popular-economicsciencesprize2024.pdf); [*Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences (2025)](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2025/10/popular-economicsciencesprize2025.pdf)
- Recommended reading: [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences (2018)](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/10/popular-economicsciencesprize2018.pdf); [Jones (2023)](https://www.kansascityfed.org/Jackson%20Hole/documents/9771/GrowthOutlookPanel.pdf)

### Lecture outline cont'd.

L5. **Household behavior**

- Labor supply and unemployment; consumption and investment
- Literature: Jones, chapters 7 and 16, appendix to 15, and parts of 17; [*Steinsson (2025b, sections 1-2 and 4-4.4)](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~jsteinsson/teaching/labor.pdf)

L6. **The short run**

- Business cycles; the IS curve; monetary policy; the Phillips curve; the IS-MP-PC framework
- Literature: Jones, chapters 8-12
- Recommended reading: [Buiter and Rahbari (2015)](https://ir.citi.com/p%2FJ9Uj80%2BaxALKreeY0Z1vUZImlOZOLlB6g4Rv8kllN5sDCBZyPvGe3ti1Rfq%2BSM)

### Lecture outline cont'd.

L7. **The short run**

- Amplification and propagation; the AS-AD model; the Taylor rule; rules and discretion
- Literature: Jones, chapters 9-13
- Recommended reading:
    [Giannone and Primiceri (2024)](https://cepr.org/voxeu/columns/drivers-post-pandemic-inflation); [Nakamura, Riblier and Steinsson (2025)](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~jsteinsson/papers/Taylor_Rule.pdf); [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences (2004)](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/advanced-economicsciences2004-1.pdf)

L8. **Monetary policy in practice**

- The transmission mechanism; financial markets in the macroeconomy; money and quantitative easing; communication and central bank independence
- Literature: Jones, chapters 10 and 14
- Recommended reading: [McLeay, Radia and Thomas (2014)](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/-/media/boe/files/quarterly-bulletin/2014/money-in-the-modern-economy-an-introduction.pdf); [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences (2022)](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2022/10/popular-economicsciencesprize2022-2.pdf)

### Lecture outline cont'd.

L9. **Fiscal policy and government debt**

- The Keynesian cross and multipliers; automatic stabilizers; Ricardian equivalence; debt sustainability; fiscal frameworks
- Literature: Jones, chapters 11 and 18

L10. **Exchange rates and international finance**

- Purchasing power parity; interest-rate parity; the open-economy IS curve and AS-AD framework; exchange-rate regimes and currency crises
- Literature: Jones, chapter 20

### Lecture outline cont'd.

L11. **The international monetary system and the balance of payments**

- The balance of payments and international capital flows; external adjustment, reserve currencies, geoeconomics
- Literature: Jones, chapter 19
- Recommended reading: [Miran (2024)](https://www.hudsonbaycapital.com/documents/FG/hudsonbay/research/638199_A_Users_Guide_to_Restructuring_the_Global_Trading_System.pdf); [Obstfeld (2025)](https://www.brookings.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/BPEA-SP25_WEB_Obstfeld.pdf), pages 139–167; [Eichengreen (2025)](https://www.cigionline.org/static/documents/Barry_Eichengreen_phCpxAb.pdf); [Bénassy-Quéré (2025)](https://www.banque-france.fr/en/governors-interventions/international-monetary-system-unfair)

L12. **Extra time and recap**
